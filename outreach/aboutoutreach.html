About Our Team

The Outreach Team is responsible for building relationships with local businesses, organizations, and sponsors who share our passion for STEM education and innovation.

We work tirelessly to secure the resources, funding, and partnerships that make Team Citrix's ambitious goals possible. From corporate sponsorships to community engagement, we are the bridge between our team and the greater community.

Key Focus Areas

  • Sponsors: Building partnerships with buisnesses.
  • Community Outreach: Promoting robotics around our community
  • Fundraising: Organizing events and campaigns to secure funding
  • Networking: Connecting with mentors and donors.
